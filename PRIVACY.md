# LocalView for Google™: Privacy Policy

LocalView does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.

- **No servers.** The extension sends nothing to the developer or anyone
  else. It has no analytics, no accounts, no ads, and no remote code.
- **Your settings** (languages, region, products, saved choices) are stored
  with Chrome's storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs them to your other
  browsers. The developer cannot see them.
- **On Google pages** the extension reads the page address only to add the
  `hl`, `lr` and `gl` language and region settings, and to show the matching
  language on the toolbar icon. This happens in your browser; nothing is
  stored or sent. It does not change your IP address or location.
- **Removing** the extension deletes its settings.

LocalView complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the
Limited Use requirements.
