Products

LocalView changes language and region only on the products you select.

Product settings

These products use their own language or region. To add one, open the popup on that product and choose “Just Maps” (or that product’s name).

    Saved choices

    Save a choice in the popup with the bookmark button. It then appears there as a one-tap button.

      About

      LocalView changes only the language and region settings that Google uses in your browser. It does not change your IP address or location. It does not collect, store, or send any data to a server. Your settings are saved with Chrome sync.
