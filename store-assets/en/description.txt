LocalView lets you choose the language and region Google uses, without a VPN and without changing your location.

- Display language: the language of Google's menus and buttons.
- Results language: show only pages written in one language.
- Region: the country whose results and prices come first.

Works on Google Search, Maps, Images, News, Shopping, Videos, Books, Flights and Hotels.

- Saved choices: save a combination and switch with one tap.
- Just Maps: give one product its own settings, for example Maps in Japanese while everything else stays in English.
- No reloads or flashes: changes apply as the page loads.
- 78 display languages, 35 results languages and 245 regions.
- The interface is available in 12 languages and follows your light or dark theme.

Privacy: LocalView collects no data. No accounts, no analytics, nothing is sent anywhere. It runs only on Google sites.
