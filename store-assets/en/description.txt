See Google the way people in another country do.

LocalView lets you choose the language Google shows, the language of your results, and the country your results come from. Your real location stays the same, and your settings apply as soon as a page loads.

How to use
1. Click the LocalView icon in the toolbar.
2. Pick a display language, results language and region.
3. Click Apply. That's it.

Features
- Saved choices: switch between combinations with one tap.
- Just Maps: give one service its own settings, like Maps in Japanese while Search stays in English.
- 78 display languages, 35 results languages and 245 regions.
- Available in 12 languages, in light and dark mode.

Privacy: LocalView collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.
